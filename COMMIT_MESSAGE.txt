Wrap the map in Atlas — national growth section with stats, charts & map (INTERNAL-PORTAL)

Atlas is Terravado's section for monitoring property growth across Australia —
macro (national & state) now, micro (region & suburb) as the finer feeds
connect. The interactive map becomes one tab inside it, alongside an Overview of
stats and charts.

What's new
- /atlas section with a tab strip: Overview and Map.
- Overview: national KPIs (pop-weighted property growth, top-growth state,
  population tracked, avg population growth), two charts (property growth by
  state; ABS population growth by state, reusing the in-house Chart), and a
  Hotspots table ranking states by property growth with population and job
  growth alongside.
- Map moved under Atlas at /atlas/map (same interactive Leaflet map, choropleth,
  overlays and development-type filter). The old /map path now redirects there.
- Nav: "Map" replaced by "Atlas".

Scope / honesty
- Macro (state) level now; region and suburb hotspots arrive with the finer
  boundaries and the connected property-data feed. Property growth is indicative
  (stub) until CoreLogic/VG; ABS figures are the labelled snapshot until the live
  ABS API is connected.

Verification
- tsc --noEmit clean
- npm run verify:engine — 23/23
- npm run build — /atlas, /atlas/map and /map (redirect) all build
