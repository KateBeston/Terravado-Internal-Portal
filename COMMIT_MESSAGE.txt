Add a depreciation modeller to the property record (INTERNAL-PORTAL)

A proper Div 43 / Div 40 depreciation modeller as a new tab on each property,
driving the engine's depreciationFor() and flowing into the Modeller's
projection and after-tax position. The old Financials tab only had an assumed
estimate with no schedule and no year-by-year output.

What's new
- /properties/[id]/depreciation tab — three bases: Assumed (estimate from
  construction cost or area x build rate + a plant allowance), Schedule (QS
  report line items), or None.
  - Div 43 capital works line editor (item, amount, years already claimed).
  - Div 40 plant & equipment line editor (asset with an effective-life
    datalist from the ATO table, value, life, prime cost / diminishing value).
  - Scrapping (year + residual written off, e.g. on a renovation).
  - Year-by-year schedule (Div 43, Div 40, scrapping, total) with year-1,
    5-year and 15-year totals and the tax saving at a selectable marginal rate.
- modelDepreciation() helper (src/lib/engine/depreciation.ts) — pure, reuses
  the engine; the tab and the Modeller agree to the dollar.
- PropertyModelling extended with div43[], div40[], scrapYear, scrapValue,
  buildRate (in the existing properties.details jsonb — no migration).
- core() (Modeller bridge) maps the schedule into the projection.
- Nav: Depreciation tab between Financials and Modelling.

Notes
- buildYear and internalArea are read from the Physical tab; Div 43 needs
  post-15-Sep-1987 construction.
- Flags (does not enforce) the 1 July 2017 second-hand plant rule for
  residential — a QS schedule is the accurate basis. Not tax advice.

Verification
- tsc --noEmit clean
- npm run verify:engine — 23/23 (new: Div 43 $320k→$8,000 y1; Div 40 DV
  $5,000/10yr→$1,000 y1)
- npm run build — depreciation tab builds
