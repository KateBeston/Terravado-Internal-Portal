feat(records): unify the person record — one tabbed Contact for borrowers and professionals

INTERNAL PORTAL.

The person record is now unified (TGS-style): Clients and Contacts are one
record. A person carries ROLES (Borrower/applicant, Broker, Buyer's agent,
Accountant, Solicitor...), and roles decide which tabs show. A "client" is
simply a person with the Borrower role.

Tabbed person record at /contacts/[id] (same chrome as the property record):
- Details    — full personal detail: name, roles, DOB, residency, marital
               status, address, ID, occupation, firm/licence/company, notes.
- Financials — the serviceability position the Modeller assesses (income,
               living expenses, dependants, commitments, card limits, HECS).
               Full income-by-type / employment / assets / liabilities detail
               lands here next.
- Properties — properties owned/considered by the person, with add + links.
- Modelling  — modelling runs saved to their file.
- Legal      — documents recorded against the person.
- Company    — their linked company (entity/role memberships come next).
Financial tabs are shown only for people with the Borrower role.

Migration 0011 (applied to live DB): adds notes/finances/details/roles to
contacts; drops the FK constraints to clients(id); copies every client into
contacts KEEPING THEIR IDs. Because the ids are preserved, every existing
client_id (properties, report snapshots, lodgements, portfolio, deal pack)
resolves to the unified record and the Modeller never breaks. The clients
table is left dormant and dropped in a later migration.

Service: client CRUD now reads/writes contacts (listClients = people with the
Borrower role); added ensureBorrower() and person-document CRUD.
Contacts list is now the full people directory and shows each person's roles.
/clients lists borrowers and deep-links into the same record; /clients/[id]
redirects to /contacts/[id].
