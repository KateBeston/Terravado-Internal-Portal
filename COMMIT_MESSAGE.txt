Land-tax completeness: foreign & trust surcharges + VIC VRLT (INTERNAL-PORTAL)

The per-state land-tax scales were already current (VIC's $50k COVID threshold,
ACT's no-threshold). The gap was the surcharges that never fired because no
entity flag triggered them. This wires them in and adds VIC's vacant-land tax.

What's new
- OwnershipEntity gains `foreign` and `deedExcludesForeign` flags, set on the
  entity record ("Land-tax status").
- aggregateLandTax now computes, per owner group: the NSW trust no-threshold
  surcharge (1.6% of the first $1.075m when a trust's deed does not exclude
  foreign persons) and the foreign-owner surcharge (the state's rate on the land
  value). The group exposes baseTax / trustSurcharge / foreignSurcharge; the
  portfolio shows each when it applies.
- Portfolio passes the entity's foreign / deed-excludes-foreign flags into the
  aggregation, so trust and foreign holdings now carry real surcharge figures
  instead of just a flag.
- vicVacantResidentialLandTax() — VIC VRLT, escalating 1%/2%/3% of capital
  improved value by consecutive vacant year (statewide from 1 Jan 2025).

Assurance: 3 new checks (NSW trust $1m → $16,000 surcharge; foreign NSW $1m →
$50,000; VIC VRLT $800k y1 → $8,000). Suite now 34/34.
