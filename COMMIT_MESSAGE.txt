Add Medicare levy thresholds, MLS and Division 293 (INTERNAL-PORTAL)

Fills the Medicare/super-tax gaps from the tax review and makes the income-tax
calc accurate at low incomes.

- Medicare levy now uses the low-income phase-in (nil below the threshold, 10%
  shade-in band, full 2% above) instead of a flat 2% — wired into incomeTax.AU,
  so it flows through every after-tax and serviceability figure. (At $100k/$200k
  the full 2% still applies, so those checks are unchanged.)
- Medicare Levy Surcharge — single tiers (1% / 1.25% / 1.5%) for those without
  private hospital cover, as medicareLevySurcharge(ti, hasCover).
- Division 293 — the extra 15% "super tax" on concessional contributions where
  income + contributions exceed $250,000, as division293(incomePlusContrib,
  concessional). Both are exported engine functions ready to wire to a
  private-cover flag and a super-contributions input.
- tax-data: MEDICARE constants (single thresholds, indexed annually — maintain
  with the tax scale).
- Assurance: 4 new known-answer checks (levy phase-in $400; levy nil; MLS $2,400;
  Div 293 $4,125). Suite now 31/31.
