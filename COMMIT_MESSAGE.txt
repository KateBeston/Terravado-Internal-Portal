feat(financials): full serviceability record — employment, income by type, assets, liabilities

INTERNAL PORTAL.

The Financials tab on the person record is now the full serviceability record,
with four detailed sections plus the assessment summary:

- Employment — employer, status (full-time/part-time/casual/contract/self-
  employed/retired/not employed), PAYG vs self-employed basis, occupation,
  industry, start date (time-in-role derived), current-role and on-probation
  flags. Multiple/previous employers supported.
- Income — one line per source, typed for serviceability: PAYG base/overtime/
  bonus/commission/allowances, self-employed (sole trader/company/trust) with
  prior-year figure + add-backs, rental, government/age pension, super/retiree
  drawdown, investment/dividends, maintenance, other. Owner (applicant/partner/
  joint), amount + frequency, annualised automatically.
- Assets — property, savings, super, vehicle, shares, business, other.
- Liabilities — home/investment loan, card, personal/car loan, HECS-HELP,
  BNPL, other, with limit, balance, repayment + frequency.
- Summary cards (income p.a., assets, liabilities, monthly repayments) and the
  assessment summary the Modeller reads today.

All stored in the contacts.finances jsonb (no migration). Finances writes now
read-merge-write so the structured lists and the summary never clobber each
other. The engine will consume the detailed lines directly as the next step.
