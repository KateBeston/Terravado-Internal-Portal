Add the CGT / disposal engine + a Sale/CGT tab (INTERNAL-PORTAL)

The biggest modelling gap from the tax review: Terravado modelled the hold but
had no sell-side. This adds a full capital-gains engine and surfaces it on the
property record.

Engine (src/lib/engine/cgt.ts) — disposalCgt():
- Capital proceeds (sale price − selling costs); cost base (purchase + acquisition
  + improvements + third-element holding costs − Div 43 claimed).
- Main residence exemption (full or partial / 6-year rule); carried-forward
  capital losses applied before the discount.
- CGT discount: 50% individuals/trusts, 1/3 SMSF, nil company — only if held
  >12 months and not a foreign resident.
- CGT payable at the owner's marginal rate / 30% company / 15% SMSF.
- Foreign resident: no discount, no main-residence exemption, and 15% FRCGW
  withheld at settlement (from 1 Jan 2025, no threshold).
- Capital-loss case carries forward. cgtEntityOf() maps an entity's kind.

UI — /properties/[id]/sale ("Sale / CGT" tab): a what-if calculator defaulted
from the record (duty via the engine, entity from ownership), with the full
cost-base-to-net-proceeds breakdown and explanatory notes.

Assurance: 4 new known-answer checks (individual 50% → $39k; company → $60k;
SMSF 1/3 → $30k; FRCGW 15% → $120k). Suite now 27/27.
