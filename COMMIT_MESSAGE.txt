Add the Shortlist engine — model-backed, per-client property ranking (INTERNAL-PORTAL)

The generalisation of stock matching into the north-star feature: rank candidate
properties for a specific client by running each through the full modelling
engine, not four preference weights. "Best on paper, for this person," explained.

What's new
- rankForClient() (src/lib/shortlist): for each candidate it runs runModel
  against the client's serviceability position and the area's growth, then ranks
  by the engine verdict — tilted by the client's goal (balanced / cashflow /
  growth) and their criteria (min yield, state, min beds). A property that isn't
  serviceable can't be a top pick. Every rank carries its working (verdict,
  serviceability, after-tax weekly, yield, projected equity).
- /shortlist page (nav "Shortlist", gated on the clients capability): pick a
  client + goal + criteria; it ranks the pool and shows the top 10 with a "Why"
  breakdown per row, the client's borrowing capacity, and the top pick.
- Candidate pool for this first slice: the tenant's "considering" properties +
  available/under-offer stock lots. The engine takes Candidate[] from any source,
  so a listings feed slots in later as another source.
- Framed as a shortlist to investigate, not advice — it models the data, not
  condition / aspect / flooding / vendor circumstances.

Verification
- tsc clean; verify:engine 23/23; build green; /shortlist → HTTP 200.
- Ran rankForClient directly on synthetic candidates: sensible ordering (cheap
  high-yield tops a cashflow goal; a $1.25m low-yield over-capacity property
  ranks last and is flagged not serviceable), with full reasons.
