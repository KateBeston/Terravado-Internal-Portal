feat(integrations): bank-data (Open Banking consumer data) seam — built and ready, gated off

INTERNAL PORTAL.

Adds the CONSUMER side of open banking as a provider seam, in the same bucket as
the other integrations (config-gated, stub fallback), ready to wire a live
provider later:

- src/lib/integrations/bank-data — BankDataProvider seam: startConnection (hosted
  consent) + getSnapshot (assessment-ready income/liability/living-expense data
  shaped to drop into a person's Financials record). Fallback "none" keeps the
  record on client-supplied statements + manual entry; a scaffolded intermediary
  provider throws a clear "build sandbox / go live via representative" message.
- Gated on BANK_DATA_PROVIDER (frollo|basiq|illion) + BANK_DATA_MODE
  (sandbox|live). Sandbox needs no accreditation and no real data; live needs the
  accredited-intermediary representative arrangement.
- Registered in the integration registry, so it shows on the Integrations page
  with its status, fallback, what it enables and how to connect.
- .env.example documents the vars.

Distinct from the existing bank-PRODUCTS seam (public product reference data, no
consent). Nothing is live; this only makes the surface ready.
