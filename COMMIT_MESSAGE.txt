feat(match): stock matching engine — rank developer stock against a client's capacity & criteria

INTERNAL PORTAL.

The property-matching wedge: score the stock list (lots) against a client's
buying position and surface the best-fit lots. Indicative ranking, not advice.

- lib/matching — matchStock(items, criteria) scores each available/under-offer
  lot on affordability (client's borrowing capacity + deposit — the strongest
  factor), yield (advertised, or derived from rent/price vs target), preferred
  state, and minimum beds; returns a 0-100 score, a verdict (strong / possible /
  stretch, never "strong" if over budget) and plain-language reasons.
- /match page (nav "Match", gated on the stock capability): pick a borrower, set
  deposit + criteria (target yield, state, min beds); borrowing capacity is
  computed from their serviceability position (assessPosition over their derived
  finances), max purchase = capacity + deposit, and the stock is ranked with
  score, verdict and the why.

No migration — reads clients, developments and lots. Next in the module: the
commission calculator, commission/revenue tracker and advisor deal pipeline.
