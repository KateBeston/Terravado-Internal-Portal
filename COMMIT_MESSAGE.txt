Shortlist: fold land tax & depreciation into the per-client ranking (INTERNAL-PORTAL)

The ranking now reflects the two factors that most change "best on paper" after
tax — depreciation and land tax — by threading them through the model so the
engine's own after-tax cashflow (and therefore the score) accounts for them.

What's new
- ModelInputs gains landValue and buildYear; core() maps them to the engine's
  land-tax basis and Div 43 eligibility — so a candidate's land tax and
  depreciation are modelled per-property, not inherited from the seed.
- Shortlist estimates, per candidate, a conservative construction cost, plant
  value and unimproved land value (clearly labelled estimates, used only when the
  record doesn't carry real figures), feeds them to runModel, and the after-tax
  weekly + verdict now include depreciation benefit and land-tax cost.
- Each row's "Why" shows the estimated year-1 depreciation and standalone land
  tax, so the numbers are explainable, not hidden in the score.

Effect
- A new build now out-ranks an identical-priced older property on after-tax
  cashflow because of depreciation (verified: ~$13k/yr yr-1 deduction, −$201/wk
  vs −$299/wk). Standalone land tax reads correctly (often $0 below threshold —
  the aggregation bite needs the client's existing holdings, a later deepening).

Verification
- tsc clean; verify:engine 23/23; build green.
- Ran rankForClient on synthetic candidates: depreciation differentiates new vs
  old; land tax computed per state; reasons surface both.
