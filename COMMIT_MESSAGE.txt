Port the equity chain into the portal (INTERNAL-PORTAL)

The last of the three features out of the retired Workspace. Models when a
client can fund their NEXT acquisition: usable equity across the owned
portfolio projected forward (the equity test) against the lender's capacity for
the new loan (the serviceability test), naming the blocker — "equity without
capacity is not a purchase".

What's new
- runEquityChain() bridge (src/lib/modelling/run.ts) — assembles a modeller
  context from the client's owned holdings (via dealInputs/core) and drives the
  engine's equityChain(). No new engine maths; reuses the verified function.
- /equity page (gated on the "portfolio" capability) — client selector,
  next-acquisition inputs (target price, deposit, costs, release LVR, yield,
  horizon, lender), go-year / equity-ready / serviceability KPIs, the blocker
  warning, and the year-by-year table (usable equity vs cash needed, capacity,
  DTI, and whether both tests pass).
- Nav entry next to Portfolio.

Notes
- Inputs are transient planning params carried in the query string, not
  persisted — the chain is a what-if.
- Equity is projected from each owned property's modelling assumptions (same
  basis as the Modeller); only properties marked "owned" are recycled.

Verification
