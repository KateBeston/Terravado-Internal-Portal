feat(account): team roles — broker-on-a-team unlocks broker features + per-page enforcement (layer 2)

INTERNAL PORTAL.

Layer 2 of the account model: team roles and team-aware capabilities.

- Adds the 'broker' team role (alongside owner/admin/adviser/viewer). A member
  with the Broker role grants the broker toolset (lender compare, finance, bank
  data, lodgement, deal export) to the WHOLE account — so a consultant who adds a
  broker to the team unlocks broker features without changing their account type.
- effectiveCapabilities(type, roles) = account-type capabilities UNION the
  capabilities granted by the team's roles. The nav and pages use this.
- Per-page enforcement: /lenders, /finance and /lodgements now call
  requireCapability() and redirect to /account if the capability is absent — so
  gating is real, not just nav-hiding.
- Account page gains a Team panel: lists members (with emails), lets an
  owner/admin change a member's role, and shows the account's effective
  capabilities (flagging when a broker on the team is the reason a feature is on).

Migration 0014 (applied): extends the memberships role check to include
'broker'; adds a memberships_update RLS policy for owners/admins (via a
non-recursive current_user_admin_tenants() helper); adds SECURITY DEFINER
functions tenant_members() (team + emails, scoped to the caller's tenant) and
current_tenant_roles() (drives team-aware capabilities).

Next (final layer, built deliberately): external guest deal-sharing —
cross-tenant, deal-scoped, with invite/accept and scoped RLS.
