feat(engine): assess serviceability from the detailed income & liability lines

INTERNAL PORTAL.

The Modeller and deal pack now derive the assessment figures from a person's
detailed Financials record instead of only the manual summary:

- deriveAssessableFinances() collapses the structured lines into the summary
  the engine already consumes, so the engine math (and its 20 assurance checks)
  is unchanged — only the inputs are better.
- Income by owner: Applicant + Joint lines total to gross income, Partner lines
  to partner income; each line annualised by its frequency. Self-employed lines
  average the latest and prior year when a prior year is given, plus add-backs.
  Income is fed at gross — lender-specific shading stays in the lender policy
  layer, so nothing is double-counted.
- Liabilities: credit-card limits, HECS-HELP balances and other repayments
  (annualised to monthly) are summed from the liability lines.
- When the structured lists are present they are authoritative; otherwise the
  stored summary fields are used as-is. The Modeller still lets you override any
  figure on the form.

Wired into the Modeller (contacts[].finances) and the deal pack.
