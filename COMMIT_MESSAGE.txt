feat(entities): unify companies + entities into one typed record with person<->entity role memberships

INTERNAL PORTAL.

Companies and ownership entities are now one record (as "a company is an
entity"). The unified Entities directory holds every company, trust, SMSF or
partnership that holds property AND every network org — agency, lender,
conveyancer, accountant, developer — each with a type, ABN, state and contact
details.

Entity record is tabbed (same chrome as the person/property records):
- Details      — name, type, state, ABN, phone/email/website, primary client.
- People & roles — who is linked and in what role (director, trustee,
  beneficiary, member, signatory...), with primary/can-sign flags.
- Properties   — properties attributed to this entity.

Person <-> entity memberships: a new entity_members join carries the role, so a
person can be director of one entity and trustee of another. The person record's
Entities tab shows and manages their memberships.

Property ownership can now be attributed to an entity (owning trust/SMSF/company
selector on the property Overview); the entity's Properties tab lists them.

Migration 0012 (applied): widens entities.kind to free text, adds phone/email/
website; folds companies into entities preserving ids (so contacts.company_id
resolves); drops the companies FK; creates entity_members (+RLS, indexes). The
companies table is left dormant. /companies and /companies/[id] redirect to the
entity record; Companies is removed from the nav.
